## Behaviour changes players will notice

- The increment chosen in the lobby applies, and players are only paired with someone who chose the same increment. A "Classical" (30 min) game updates the classical rating (was rapid). Private "Rapid"/"Blitz"/… games play at that speed (were all 10 minutes).
- A game in which a side does not make its first move within 30 s is aborted, unrated.
- Leaving before both sides have moved aborts the game instead of hanging (or, after this branch, instead of a rated win).
- A move sent after your flag fell is refused and you lose on time immediately.
- While a player is disconnected both clocks stand still for everyone (they used to keep falling on screen, twice as fast).
- Closing one of two tabs on the same game no longer starts the "opponent disconnected" countdown.
- Chat messages are stored (they were stored empty) and chat closes when the game ends.
- Refused actions (resign in a finished game, a chat message after the game, a bad queue join) show a short message.
- Roughly once an hour the game socket reconnects to pick up a fresh token; the board re-syncs.
- An old invite link to a game that already started shows "not found or expired".
- A game older than 2 hours is no longer auto-abandoned while it is still being played.
